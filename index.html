A Little About Me 🌿

Originally from Florida’s Forgotten Coast and now creating along the quiet shores of Kanagawa, Japan. My art reflects a colorful mind across multiple tactile mediums.

📿

Intricate Beadwork

Using traditional brick stitch and fine Miyuki beads to construct lightweight, striking geometric fringe earrings and wearable art.

🧶

Needle Felting

Sculpting soft wool fibers into organic nature motifs—ginkgo leaves, birds, and floral accents for hats, ties, and brooches.

🎨

Mixed Media Art

Capturing spontaneous perspectives through analog photography, rich oil pastels, and fluid watercolor paintings.
