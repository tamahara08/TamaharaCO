About the artist

I am originally from the forgotten coast in the Florida Panhandle. I grew up always loving art and creating things with my hands, dreaming of one day having my own business. I remember doing photoshoots on my mom’s iPad, staging whatever I had made, or staying awake at absurd hours of the night trying to hand-sew long tulle and lace to my jeans so I could show my friends at school the next day, hoping they’d let me customize their clothes. Over time and across different mediums, I’ve found the most joy in working with seed beads using brick-stitch techniques, along with recent explorations in needle felting—always returning to where the love started: painting and illustrating. Since I’m still in my 20s, I’m still finding who I truly am as an artist and which stones figuratively I want to build my house out of... which at this rate will probably be a house of many colors and patterns, just like an American quilt. Now that I’ve moved to Japan, I look forward to what this chapter will create. I just know I’ll be happy to have your support along the way, because you can be certain I will share my art with you.
— Yours truly, Jade
