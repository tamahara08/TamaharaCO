Background & Craft

My Story

Hello! I'm a stay-at-home artist and jewelry craftsperson rooted in Ebina, Kanagawa. My creative practice is an exploration of slow, tactile work—transforming tiny glass beads and soft wool into wearable pieces of nature.

Whether I'm constructing custom fringe earrings with precise Miyuki beads using the brick stitch method, or needle-felting ginkgo leaves, botanical patterns, and birds onto hats and brooches, every piece is made with patience, care, and an appreciation for analog craft.
